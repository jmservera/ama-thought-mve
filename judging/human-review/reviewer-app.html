Scoring guidance

Does expected equal actual mean a 4?

A matching comparison is evidence that this check passed, not an automatic rating for every dimension. A 4 is appropriate when that dimension's exercised requirements are coherently fulfilled. Use NA when the dimension is unobserved, such as recovery with no demonstrated subsequent progress. Do not invent defects to avoid a 4, or infer untested capabilities from a pass.
